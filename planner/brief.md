# Trip brief

I will be in Tokyo 15–19 Dec, Hakone 19–21, Osaka 21–26 (Osaka base, but during the day we will
commute to Kyoto/Nara as needed, ending the night/nightlife in Osaka; if there are cool nightlife
things to do in the neighbouring cities as well, include them), then Tokyo again 26–29 Dec 2026.

We land at Haneda Terminal 3 on Tue 15 Dec around 15:00 and fly home from Haneda Terminal 3 on
Tue 29 Dec at 17:20.

We are a group of 8, all around 20 years old, all Asian. We love to drink, and we want to maximise
all the time we've got in the country. Think about things like Round1, shopping, and so on; we love
to do it all. No go-karts. Look for money we can save by booking early or buying passes.

Stays (already booked): Ninja House in Toshima (Tokyo), Hot Spring Hakone Villa in Sengokuhara,
Kujō House in Nishi-ku (Osaka), Garfield House in Shinjuku (Tokyo).

## What would make this trip legendary

We are eight degenerates, and the goal is the story we tell on the flight home, not a box ticked.
A great day here has one moment nobody else we know has done, with good food and drinks around it.
We already wrote a plan ("Plan C", in data/japan-plan-c.md) that is the real us. Treat it as the
spine: keep what survives checking, and replace what does not with something equally special.

Anti-goals. Nothing that is only there because it is on page one of "Tokyo itinerary" or "Kyoto in
a day". No sit-down sightseeing filler between the big moments. No nightclubs (a group rule from
Plan C; ignore touts and "bar" invites in Kabukichō). No go-karts.

Be bold. Prefer one-of-a-kind, local-run, hard-to-book and group-scale things (private rooms, a
table for 8, events that only happen on these dates). For every hard-to-book thing, name a safe
fallback beside it so a sold-out ticket does not wreck the day.

- Christmas Day in Santa suits, the whole group, ending in a Round1 all-nighter in Osaka.
- The Grand Santa Parade in Umekita, Osaka (about 500 Santas, suits provided and yours to keep; Plan C had it on Tue 22 Dec). Needs a sign-up.
- Pro wrestling: Pro Wrestling NOAH "Thanksgiving 2026" at Korakuen Hall (Plan C had Fri 18 Dec).
- A B.LEAGUE basketball game, Tokyo Sunrockers at Toyota Arena Tokyo (Plan C had Sun 27 Dec).
- A yakatabune dinner cruise with unlimited drinks on Sat 26 Dec, the night of the Odaiba fireworks.
- Hiroshima and Miyajima (floating torii, grilled oysters, okonomiyaki) if a day can be spared; otherwise Kyoto and Nara carry the Kansai days.
- Yunessun wine, sake and coffee baths in Hakone, and a villa wagyu cook-off on the Hakone night.
- A KFC Christmas party barrel on Christmas Eve.
- Fugu in Osaka.
- Morning sumo practice at a stable (Plan C: Arashio-beya in Ryōgoku) and a private omakase counter.
- A 24-hour onsen night (Thermae-yu in Kabukichō or Spa World in Shinsekai).

## How we like to travel

Streetwear, sneakers and vintage (Harajuku, Koenji, Shimokitazawa, Ameyoko, Amerikamura), Don Quijote
and tax-free hauls, arcades, karaoke (Manekineko, Big Echo, Karaokekan) and Round1. Izakaya
and yokocho nights, standing bars, and the last-train problem solved properly. Keep the long
nights, but not every night: at most two deliberate nights under 4 hours' sleep, and any early start
the day after a late night needs a reason (rope-drop at USJ, the first Fushimi Inari light). Everyone
stays together, and every booking says "8名で、同じテーブル/個室".

Never book, pay or post anything. Every booking becomes a to-do with a due date and how to do it.
